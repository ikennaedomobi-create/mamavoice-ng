# MamaVoice NG 🇳🇬

MamaVoice is a free, mobile-first web app that helps pregnant women and new mothers in Nigeria understand danger signs, get antenatal care reminders, and find help fast.

Built for low-literacy, low-data, and low-connectivity communities in Imo State and across Nigeria.

Live App: https://ikennaedomobi-create.github.io/mamavoice-ng/

## Problem
Many mothers in Nigeria die from preventable causes because they don't recognise danger signs early, or don't know where to go for help.

## What MamaVoice Does
- Explains danger signs in simple language
- Antenatal care (ANC) reminders
- Emergency help: What to do + Where to go
- Works on any phone, no app download needed
- Works in English, Pidgin, and local languages

## Who Is It For?
- Pregnant women
- New mothers
- Community Health Workers
- Husbands and families

## Tech
Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Hosted free on GitHub Pages.

## Vision
To partner with UNICEF Nigeria, Primary Health Care, and community leaders to reach 1 million mothers with life-saving information.
